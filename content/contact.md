# Contact

The fastest way to reach the BotStopper team is email:

- General, organizational, and licensing questions:
  [me@xeiaso.net](mailto:me@xeiaso.net)

When you write, it helps to include:

- What you are trying to protect (a site, an API, a service).
- How you deploy today (binary packages, Docker, Podman, Kubernetes).
- What automated traffic you are seeing, if you have examples.

For bug reports and feature requests, prefer the issue trackers on GitHub
under [TecharoHQ](https://github.com/TecharoHQ) so the discussion is
public and searchable.

If you want to fund the work, BotStopper is sponsored through
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Xe).
