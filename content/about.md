# About

BotStopper is made by Techaro, the team behind
[Anubis](https://anubis.techaro.lol), the open source proof-of-work bot
protection tool that many websites run behind. BotStopper brings the same
practical approach to automated traffic into a product designed for
organizations: open source roots, calmer defaults, and documentation a
team can actually deploy from.

The people behind BotStopper have spent years fighting unwanted automated
traffic without making websites hostile to humans. That experience shapes
the defaults: corporate-friendly branding you can replace with your own,
challenges that do the job without drama, and headless browser detection
that runs quietly in the background.

- Website: https://botstopper.net
- Source code: https://github.com/TecharoHQ
- Upstream Anubis documentation: https://anubis.techaro.lol/docs
