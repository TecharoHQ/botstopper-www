# BotStopper

BotStopper from Techaro is a smarter front door for the modern web. It
helps protect websites from scraping and automated abuse while keeping the
experience straightforward for people. It is built by the same team behind
Anubis and brings that practical approach to automated traffic into a
product designed for organizations.

Good traffic gets through. Bad bots don't.

## What it does

- A proven open source foundation: built by the creators of Anubis.
- Your brand at the front door: start with a calm, corporate-friendly
  default experience, then tailor the challenge and presentation to match
  your organization.
- Headless browser detection: identify browser automation signals and
  apply friction where it helps protect your site and its resources.
- A hybrid challenge architecture: combine signals and challenges to
  distinguish useful visitors from unwanted automated traffic.
